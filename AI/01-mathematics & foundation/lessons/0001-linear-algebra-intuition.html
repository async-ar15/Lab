Lesson 01: Linear Algebra Intuition

Every AI model is just matrix math wearing a fancy hat.

The Fundamental Disconnect

Computers do not understand concepts. They do not know what a "cat" is, what "sadness" feels like, or what a "house" looks like. Computers only process numbers.

To build Artificial Intelligence, we must bridge the gap between the physical world of concepts and the digital world of computation. Linear Algebra is the bridge. It is the mathematical language we use to translate reality into a format a computer can calculate.

First Principle 1: Vectors are Concepts

A vector is traditionally taught as "an arrow with direction and magnitude." Erase that from your mind. In AI, a vector is simply a list of numbers that represents a concept.

Imagine a 2D space where the X-axis is "How fluffy is it?" and the Y-axis is "How dangerous is it?".

  • A Golden Retriever might be the vector [0.9, 0.1] (very fluffy, not dangerous).
  • A Crocodile might be the vector [0.0, 0.9] (not fluffy, very dangerous).

By mapping concepts to numbers, we place them in a geometric space. Suddenly, we can do math on concepts. We can measure the distance between a Dog and a Crocodile. In a Large Language Model (LLM), words are mapped to vectors with 4,096 dimensions. This is called an Embedding.

First Principle 2: The Dot Product is Similarity

Once concepts are mapped to vectors in space, we need a way to ask the computer: "Are these two concepts similar?"

The Dot Product is the mathematical engine of similarity. To calculate it, you multiply the matching coordinates and add them up.

Vector A (Dog) = [0.9, 0.1]
Vector B (Cat) = [0.8, 0.2]

Dot Product = (0.9 * 0.8) + (0.1 * 0.2)
            = 0.72 + 0.02
            = 0.74 (High Similarity)

If two vectors point in the exact same direction, their dot product is large and positive. If they are perpendicular (unrelated), their dot product is zero. If they point in opposite directions, it is negative.

Where this is used: When you ask ChatGPT a question, it converts your question into a vector, and takes the dot product against millions of document vectors. The documents with the highest dot products are retrieved. This is the exact math behind RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and Attention mechanisms.

First Principle 3: Matrices are Transformations

If vectors are data, Matrices are the actions performed on that data.

A matrix is a grid of numbers. When you multiply a matrix by a vector, it physically alters the vector's position in space. It can stretch it, rotate it, squish it, or project it into a completely different dimension.

Every single layer of a neural network is a matrix. When data (a vector) passes through a neural network, it is repeatedly multiplied by weight matrices. The neural network is literally reshaping the space the data lives in, untangling the complex concepts until a straight line can separate the "Dogs" from the "Cats".

output = weights @ input

(The @ symbol in Python is matrix multiplication).
