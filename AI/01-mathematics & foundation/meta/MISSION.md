# Mission

**Mission Statement:** To learn Artificial Intelligence Engineering from scratch, starting with the absolute core foundational math, using first-principles thinking to eliminate fluff and build deep, structural understanding.

## Why are we doing this?
To transition from writing simple logic to understanding and building systems that learn from data. Machine learning is purely applied mathematics at an enormous scale. Without mathematical intuition, AI is a black box; with mathematical intuition, AI is just geometry and calculus.

## Current Focus
Phase 1: Math Foundations
Sub-phase: Linear Algebra (The Language of Data)
